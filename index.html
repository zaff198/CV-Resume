LOCUS 01 About

Plant geneticist, statistician, builder of tools.

I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Genetics & Plant Breeding at SKUAST‑Kashmir, completing my thesis on genome‑wide association studies (GWAS) for yield and yield‑attributing traits in temperate rice. My work sits at the intersection of quantitative genetics, population genomics, and applied statistics — translating dense marker‑trait data into breeding decisions that hold up in the field.

Alongside my doctoral research, I run Data Harvest Labs, an MSME‑registered analytics practice delivering bioinformatics, biostatistics, and data science support to researchers across agricultural, medical, veterinary, and life‑science disciplines. I also serve as a Research Analyst at Cashmir Biotech Pvt. Ltd. I write R packages and Shiny applications that turn multi‑step statistical pipelines — GWAS summary mapping, MET stability analysis, PCA — into single‑click tools other researchers can actually use.

My broader research footprint spans diversity analysis in rice, oats, and common bean; root phenomics in buckwheat; nutritional genomics and biofortification (Vit‑GWAS, Fe/Zn dynamics); plant–microbe interactions; and reactive‑oxygen‑species stress physiology — reflected across 14+ publications, a filed patent on an apple‑scab biocontrol agent, and ongoing peer review for journals including BMC Biology.
